One Word Later privacy policy

Kunal Tandon publishes One Word Later for Android. You can play without creating an account.

Game data and age screening

One Word Later stores your settings, Classic progress, Daily completion, statistics, birth year, age-screening result and coin history in app storage. Twin Play chains are held for the match. The game uses the youngest possible age for your birth year to select appropriate ad and consent settings. It sends age-treatment flags to the advertising SDK. Android device backup and transfer settings can affect copies of your app data.

Advertising and choices

One Word Later uses Google AdMob. After age screening, the game requests non-personalized ads. Players treated as children can receive child-directed, G-rated banners. Rewarded and interstitial ads are available only to eligible older players. Other requests have a PG limit, with additional restrictions for younger players. In regions where Google provides a consent form, the game uses Google's consent tools and makes available privacy choices accessible in Settings.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK can process IP addresses, device identifiers, interactions with ads and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. What it receives depends on age treatment, consent, device settings and SDK behavior. The one-time Remove ads purchase turns off banners and automatic interstitials. Eligible players can still choose a rewarded ad to earn coins.

How Google uses information from partner apps

Store purchases

Google Play processes payments. One Word Later receives product and transaction information and stores purchase references, the coin balance and Remove ads ownership in app storage. The game checks purchases with Google Play to deliver coin packs and restore Remove ads for the same store account. The app does not receive your payment card details.

Deleting data and contacting us

Clearing One Word Later's app storage removes the local save, including progress and coins. Uninstalling the game also removes the local save. Android may restore a save from a device backup. Google Play manages purchase records in your Google account. For privacy questions or a request about a child's data, contact Kunal Tandon at kunaltandon.kt@gmail.com.
