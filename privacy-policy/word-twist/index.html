Word Twist privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

Kunal Tandon publishes Word Twist for Android. This notice explains how this app handles information. For privacy questions, including questions from a parent or guardian, email Kunal Tandon at kunaltandon.kt@gmail.com.

Playing the game

You can solve puzzles offline without creating an account. Word Twist saves your puzzle progress, statistics, coin balance, purchase entitlements and settings in the app’s storage on your device. The game uses this information to resume play, display your statistics and keep track of hints and rewards. Gameplay events used by the game itself remain in memory during a session. Android backup or device transfer may copy app data according to your device settings.

Advertising

Word Twist uses Google AdMob to display banners on game and menu screens, show ads between certain completed puzzles and offer optional videos that award hint coins. When an internet connection is available, the app checks Google’s User Messaging Platform for the applicable privacy settings. The app can request ads in advance once the SDK permits it.

In connection with advertising, Google may receive your IP address and infer a general location, record app and ad interactions such as launches and video views, and process performance information and device or app identifiers. These can include the app set ID for purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention. Google uses information to deliver ads, measure activity and protect its services against abuse. Its mobile ads SDK encrypts this traffic in transit. Google’s mobile ads SDK disclosures

Word Twist is intended for players age 13 and over. The game does not ask for a birth date. It requests non-personalized ads with a maximum content rating of PG and applies under-age privacy settings to ad requests and privacy messaging for all players. When Google’s privacy service offers choices, you can revisit them under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Buying Remove ads hides banners and stops ads between puzzles. You can still choose to watch a video for hint coins. The game may load a reward video in advance when Google permits ad requests, including after a Remove ads purchase.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles checkout for coin packs and Remove ads. Word Twist receives the product, quantity, purchase status and a purchase token. The app saves tokens on your device to prevent duplicate coin grants and to keep track of your Remove ads purchase. It queries Google Play for owned purchases and sends transaction confirmations to Play. Payment details such as card numbers are entered in Google Play. Google handles its records according to its own privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy

Retention, deletion and help

The game keeps your saved progress and purchase records on the device until its app storage is cleared. To erase the local copy, open Android Settings → Apps → Word Twist → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes local coins and progress. A current Remove ads purchase can be restored from Google Play with the purchasing account. Previously consumed coin packs cannot be restored from Play.

Google maintains its own ad and purchase records. Clearing Word Twist storage does not erase those records or a device backup. You can manage Google account data and privacy requests through Google’s privacy controls. To ask about information held by the publisher or exercise rights available in your region, use the publisher contact above. Google privacy controls

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and retain it as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your inquiry or meet legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion, email kunaltandon.kt@gmail.com and identify Word Twist in your message. We may ask for information needed to identify the record and verify your request.
