Fun Ways to Think Privacy Policy

Kunal Tandon publishes Fun Ways to Think for Android on Google Play under the package com.funwaystothink.kunaltandon. This policy applies to that game, which is designed for children, teens, and adults.

Game progress and age screening

The game saves puzzle progress, coins, settings, and purchase entitlements in its local storage. It has no player accounts or cloud save service. Its production build has no separate gameplay analytics service.

On first launch, the game asks for your birth year to choose age-appropriate ad settings. It uses the year to determine whether it can establish that the player is an adult. The game keeps the year and age group in local storage. It does not send your birth year to us, Google, or advertisers. Google receives an under-age setting when the game checks advertising privacy requirements for younger players. The game leaves advertising off while age is unknown.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob to serve a labeled banner when Google's User Messaging Platform indicates that ad requests are allowed. For children and teens, the game requests child-directed, non-personalized banners and uses Google's under-age privacy setting. Google’s child-directed ad setting disables interest-based advertising and prevents transmission of the Android advertising ID with the ad request. Younger players are offered no rewarded ads.

Eligible adults can choose to watch an ad for game coins. Adult advertising depends on your region and applicable privacy choices. All ad requests use a G content rating cap. The built-in Advertising privacy choices entry in Settings appears where Google's privacy system requires it for adults. You can also manage your device advertising ID in Android settings. Playing the puzzles does not require watching rewarded ads.

When its advertising or consent services are used, Google may use the device’s IP address to estimate approximate location. Depending on the request and privacy settings, Google may also process device or app identifiers, interactions such as app launches and ad views, consent signals, and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, privacy compliance, and fraud prevention. For how Google handles this information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Google Play processes payments and manages your Google Play account and payment information under its privacy policy. The game receives purchase details, including product and transaction identifiers, purchase time, and the purchase token. It stores purchase records locally to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate grants, and restore supported purchases. The game does not receive payment card details. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the gameplay banner.

Retention, security, and your choices

Game information is kept with the app on your device. To remove the local copy, use Android's option to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage may remove your progress and unspent coins. Google Play maintains its own purchase records, and eligible permanent purchases may be restored through the game.

If you email us for support or to exercise a privacy right, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and resolve the request. We keep this information only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, unless the law requires longer retention. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information we handle.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, contact Kunal Tandon at kunaltandon.kt@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify the request. Parents and guardians can also use this address to ask about the game or request deletion of information a child sent to us.
